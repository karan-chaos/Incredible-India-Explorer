About the Data

The speaker population figures and language families presented in this visualization are derived from the Census of India 2011, the most recent comprehensive linguistic survey conducted by the Government of India. Languages are grouped by their primary linguistic family. Note that "Hindi" in this context includes related dialects counted under the Hindi umbrella in the census.
