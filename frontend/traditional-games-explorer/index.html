India's Gaming Legacy

Games That Conquered the World

India is the undisputed birthplace of the world's most popular strategy and race games. Chaturanga became Chess. Pachisi became Ludo. Pallankuzhi influenced Mancala. Gilli-danda inspired Cricket and Baseball. The games born in Indian villages and royal courts now entertain billions worldwide.

♟️
Chaturanga → Chess

6th century CE, Gupta Empire. The "four divisions" of the army gave us the world's most popular strategy game.

🎲
Pachisi → Ludo

4th century CE. Mughal emperors played on marble courts at Fatehpur Sikri. Now played in every household globally.

🏏
Gilli-danda → Cricket

Ancient village game. The principles of bat-and-ball play originated here before British colonial adaptation.

🏁
Pallankuzhi → Mancala

Over 2,000 years old. This pit-and-seed counting game spread from South India across Africa and the world.
